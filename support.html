Purchase and restore

  1. In app settings, refresh products and confirm you are signed in with the Apple ID used for purchase.
  2. Choose Restore Purchases and allow the App Store to sync transactions.
  3. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple ID subscription settings. The App Store price and cycle shown at purchase apply.

Notification, microphone, and speech recognition

  • Notification: allow notifications for AI-native Todo in iOS Settings, then enable reminders in the app for tasks with a due time.
  • Microphone: if voice input cannot record, allow microphone access in iOS Settings, restart the app, and try again.
  • Speech recognition: allow speech recognition in iOS Settings, check network and system-language availability, and review transcribed text before sending.
